What changed on 4 November 2026

  • App lock with your phone's fingerprint/face/screen lock, or an app PIN stored only as a salted hash in your phone's secure storage (never sent to our servers); Forgot PIN via your account password or a reset email.
  • Admin activity log: admin moderation actions are recorded (admin ID and email, time, target, reason), kept without expiry for accountability, admin-only.
  • Flags, the honesty pledge (pledgeAcceptedAt) and Terms acceptance (termsVersion, termsAcceptedAt) records are described.
  • All posts are deleted after about 2 years; a profanity filter blocks abusive words.
