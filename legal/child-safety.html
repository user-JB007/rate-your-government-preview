Child safety standards

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Rate Your Government (app com.bhagath.rategov, developer Bhagath J) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Our standards

  • The app is for adults aged 18 and over only.
  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children is strictly prohibited, including images, videos, text, links and grooming or solicitation.
  • Accounts that post or share such content are permanently banned, and the content is removed.
  • Confirmed CSAM is reported to the relevant authorities, including India's National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and NCMEC where applicable, and evidence is preserved as required by law.

How to report

  • In the app: open the post and use the Flag / Report option. Flagged content is reviewed by moderators, and harmful content is removed as quickly as possible.
  • By email: write to helpme.dev@outlook.com with the subject "Child safety", along with a description or link.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 112 or the Childline on 1098, or report at cybercrime.gov.in.

Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws, including India's POCSO Act, 2012, and the IT Act, 2000, and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

Point of contact
