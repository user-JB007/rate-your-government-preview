Grievance Officer & takedown requests

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Last updated: 4 November 2026

Under the Information Technology Act, 2000, the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can raise complaints about content or about how your data is handled.

What to send

  • Your name and contact email (for data requests, write from your registered email).
  • The username, place, and date of the content, plus a screenshot if possible.
  • What is wrong, for example hate speech, defamation or false claim, personal information, obscene or violent content, impersonation, copyright, or a privacy or data request.
  • For legal orders: a copy of the order.

Timelines

  • We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours where feasible.
  • We resolve them within 15 days of receipt.
  • Complaints about content showing a person's private area, nudity or sexual acts, or impersonation of a person in such content, are acted on within 24 hours.
  • Takedown orders from a court or an authorised government agency are acted on within the time the law sets.

Quick reporting in the app

You can also tap Flag on any report and choose a reason. Flagged content is reviewed by the admin. Each flag records your user ID, the reason and the time, and is visible only to the admin. Every admin decision (who, when, what and why) is kept in an admin activity log, so you can ask us to explain a moderation decision.

App PIN and account access

If you forgot your app PIN, use Forgot PIN? on the lock screen and confirm your account password. If you also forgot your password, choose the reset-email option there. We cannot see or recover your PIN, because it is stored only as a salted hash on your phone. Contact: helpme.dev@outlook.com.

If you are not satisfied with our response to a data-protection grievance, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
