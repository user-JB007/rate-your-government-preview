Delete your account and data

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Rate Your Government account (app com.bhagath.rategov, developer Bhagath J) and the data linked to it.

Option 1: Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Open the Menu and tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm. You may be asked to confirm your password.

Option 2: Ask by email

If you can't use the app, email helpme.dev@outlook.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my account". Include your username. We will confirm and delete it within 15 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and linked mobile number) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your private profile, public profile and username.
  • All your ratings and reports, and the data stored on your device by the app.

What may be kept

  • Photo and video files of your reports on Cloudinary are removed manually and may take up to 30 days to disappear.
  • Flags you made on other people's reports may remain, without your profile, until those reports expire.
  • Limited records we must keep by law, for example under a legal order, are kept only for as long as required.
  • Backups that Google Firebase keeps for its own operations expire on their own schedule.
