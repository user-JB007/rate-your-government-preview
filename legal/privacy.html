Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Rate Your Government app ("the app", "we") collects, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and your rights. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000. The app is run by Bhagath J, an individual developer based in Maddur, Karnataka, India (the "Data Fiduciary").

1. Data we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password). Email verification is mandatory.
  • Mobile number (optional): if you choose to link it, it is verified with a one-time SMS code through Firebase Phone Authentication. Mobile number verification is currently optional. In a future update it will become mandatory to keep posting; we'll notify you in the app before that happens.
  • Profile: your name, a public username you choose, home State/UT, the place you set in your profile, and optionally your birthday. A profile photo, if you add one, stays on your device.
  • Location (GPS): used when you post, to find which State/UT you are in (you can only rate or report places where you are) and to tag reports with where they were taken.
  • Ratings and reports: star ratings, comments, report category, and for reports up to 5 photos and one 10-second video with sound (videos include sound from the scene and may capture people's voices; the microphone is used only while you record, with your permission), taken only with the in-app camera and tagged with the time and GPS location.
  • Flags: if you flag content, we store your user ID, the reported item, the reason you chose and the time. Flags are visible only to the admin and are used for moderation.
  • Consent records: the version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted and when (termsVersion, termsAcceptedAt), and, for each rating or report, the time you ticked the honesty pledge (pledgeAcceptedAt: "I confirm this is my honest experience, factual, and not hateful or defamatory").
  • Admin activity log: when an admin acts on content or an account (for example hiding or removing a post, resolving a flag, suspending or restoring a user), we record the admin's user ID and email, the action, the time, the target item or account, and the reason given. If you are the target, your user ID or post ID appears in that record.
  • App lock: the app can be locked with your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock (handled entirely by your phone; we never receive biometric data), or, if your phone has none or you prefer, with a 4–6 digit app PIN. The app PIN is stored only on your phone, as a salted hash in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). It is never sent to our servers, and it is erased when you sign out. If you forget it, you reset it by re-entering your account password, or by requesting a password-reset email and signing in again.
  • Technical data: basic data that Firebase collects by default to run its services (such as device and connection information and error logs).

We do not collect your Aadhaar number or any other government ID number. There are no ads, no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics beyond Firebase's defaults. We do not sell or rent your data.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and verify your email and (optionally) mobile number.
  • To publish your ratings and reports under your username, and show where reports were taken.
  • To check that ratings come from the place being rated, and to limit fake or duplicate posts.
  • To moderate content, handle flags, complaints and legal requests, and prevent abuse. An automatic filter blocks posts containing common abusive or profane words before they are saved.
  • To keep an accountable record of admin moderation actions, so decisions can be checked and we can meet our legal duties as an intermediary.
  • To keep the app private on your phone with the app lock.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you sign up and accept these terms, and for legitimate uses allowed by law (such as complying with legal orders).

3. What is public

The public feed shows your username, your ratings, comments, report photos and videos, and their time and location. Your email, mobile number, name and birthday are never shown publicly. Please don't type personal details into comments.

4. Where data is stored

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), with Firestore data in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region. Google acts as our data processor. See Firebase privacy.
  • Cloudinary stores report photos and videos. Cloudinary may store data outside India. See Cloudinary privacy.

5. How long we keep it

  • All ratings and reports (text-only, with photos, or with a video) are deleted automatically after about 2 years.
  • Photo and video files on Cloudinary are removed manually and may remain for a while after the report is gone.
  • Flags are kept while the flagged post exists and are deleted with it.
  • Your Terms acceptance records are kept with your profile; the pledge time is kept with each post.
  • The admin activity log is kept without a fixed expiry, for accountability and legal compliance (for example to answer a grievance, a Grievance Appellate Committee or a court). Only admins can read it; it cannot be edited or deleted from the app, and it is not deleted when an account is deleted.
  • The app PIN stays only on your phone until you sign out, change it or uninstall the app.
  • Your account and profile are kept until you delete your account, or we remove it for breaking the Terms of Use.
  • We may keep limited records longer if the law requires it, for example to respond to a court or government order.

6. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers above, so the app can work, and with authorities when Indian law requires it. Public posts can be seen by anyone using the app or the website.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can: get a summary of your data; correct or update it (most of it you can edit in the app); delete your account and data (how); withdraw consent (by deleting your account); nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to; and raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied. Email helpme.dev@outlook.com from your registered email address.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults. If you are under 18, use it only with the verifiable consent and supervision of your parent or lawful guardian; otherwise please do not use it. We do not knowingly track children or target them with any advertising. If we learn a child is using the app without consent, we will delete the account.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access is limited by security rules so only you can read your private profile and only admins can read flags and the admin log. The app lock (device lock or app PIN) adds protection if someone else picks up your phone; the app PIN is never stored in readable form. No system is fully secure. If a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. If the change is significant, the app will ask you to accept it again. The effective date above shows the latest version.
