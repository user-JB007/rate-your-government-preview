Is this a government app?

No. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for any government, ministry, department, local body, political party or the United Nations. Official grievance portals are run separately by the government; the app only helps you open them.

What is Rate Your Government?

An independent citizen app where people rate Union, State/UT and local governments and report problems with public services (roads, water, electricity, garbage and more). Ratings and reports are the opinions of the users who post them, not of the developer.

Who built it and how do I contact you?

Developed by Bhagath J, Maddur, Karnataka, India. Public contact: helpme.dev@outlook.com. For content or data grievances see the Grievance Officer page (linked from Legal / Privacy).

Can I donate through the app?

No. The app has no donations, payments or in-app purchases.

Is it available on iPhone?

The project is prepared for iOS (bundle ID com.bhagath.rategov), but current public builds are Android APKs via EAS. An iOS release needs a Mac, an Apple Developer account and a finished Firebase iOS setup.

What languages does the app support?

The interface is in English. You can write ratings and reports in any language your keyboard supports. An automatic filter blocks some common abusive words in English and common Hindi/Kannada transliterations.

Account & sign-in

How do I create an account?

On the sign-in screen choose Create account, enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters, then confirm the password. You must verify your email before you can finish setup and post.

How does email verification work?

After sign-up the app asks you to open the verification link sent to your email. Until the email is verified you cannot complete profile setup or post. You can resend the email from the verification screen.

Is mobile (SMS) verification required?

Not right now. Linking a mobile number by one-time SMS is optional (you can skip). A future update may make it mandatory before posting. Email verification remains required.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and enter your email. If an account exists, a reset link is sent (check inbox and spam). You can also request a reset from Forgot PIN? on the app lock screen.

What is the public username?

Your username (3–20 characters: a–z, 0–9, underscore or dot) is the only identity shown with your ratings and reports. Your email, phone, full name and birthday stay private. You can change the username later; reserved names such as admin or official are blocked. Your posts are updated to show the new username.

What is the honesty pledge and Terms acceptance?

Before posting you must tick a pledge that the post is your honest experience, factual, and not hateful or defamatory. When Terms or Privacy change materially, the app asks you to accept the new version again (termsVersion). Posts store when you accepted the pledge.

How do I delete my account?

Menu → Account → Delete my account, and confirm (you may need your password). That removes your cloud profile, username claim, ratings and reports. There is also a Delete account web page under Legal. Photo/video files on Cloudinary may remain briefly until removed manually.

App lock & privacy on the phone

What is the app lock / app PIN?

After sign-in you can secure the app with your phone’s biometric/screen lock or an app-specific PIN. The PIN is stored only on your phone as a salted hash — never sent to our servers — and is cleared when you sign out.

I forgot my app PIN.

On the lock screen tap Forgot PIN? and confirm with your account password. If you also forgot the password, request a password-reset email from there. We cannot recover your PIN.

Ratings

What can I rate?

You pick a place in the government hierarchy: Union; State or Union Territory; then district → sub-district (taluk/tehsil/mandal) → village, or a city/town urban local body. You can also rate some departments. Global/UN-style ratings exist for ratings only — reports are for local problems and cannot use Global.

How does location / GPS affect rating and reporting?

The app asks for location permission and uses a fresh GPS fix (plus reverse geocode) to detect your State/UT. For State-level and lower places, posting is locked to being inside that State (a speed bump against remote posting, not proof against spoofing). Union can be used more broadly. If permission is denied or services are off, you will see a clear error and cannot finish a geotagged report photo/video until location works.

Can I search departments?

Yes. When choosing a department you can search by keywords (for example roads, health, water).

Can I edit or delete my own rating?

Yes. Open your rating from My ratings / the feed and use Edit or Delete. Only you can edit your scores and comment; admins can delete abusive posts.

Reports (photos & video)

How do I report a problem?

Choose where the problem is (current GPS place or pick from the list), optional department, category (road not built, pothole, water, electricity, garbage, other), write a comment, add evidence with the in-app camera, tick the honesty pledge, and submit. Shared reports appear in the community feed.

Why only the in-app camera — no gallery?

Photos and the optional video must be taken live inside the app so each one carries capture time and GPS. There is no gallery or file picker anywhere in the app (including profile photos).

What about video?

One optional clip, up to about 10 seconds, recorded in-app at 720p. Videos include sound from the scene (microphone permission is asked the first time you record; if you deny mic, recording continues without sound). Clips over the size limit are rejected.

Where are photos and videos stored?

When Cloudinary is configured in the build, they upload to Cloudinary (folder for reports) and the public links are stored with the report. Text and metadata live in Firebase/Firestore (asia-south1). If an upload fails, the report text can still be public while the file stays on your phone as “pending”.

My report says the photo is not uploaded yet. What do I do?

Open the report (or My reports / reopen the app). The app retries pending uploads automatically. You can also tap Retry upload. This only works on the same phone that still has the photo file. After a successful upload, others can see the photo.

Can I edit or delete my report?

Yes. As the author you can edit the comment and category, or delete the report. Admins can delete reports that break the rules.

Send to department

What does “Send to department” do?

It does not file an official complaint. A confirmation screen shows the department/body, the portal name and full URL (or the official email for Email department), the source link, and a preview of the complaint text (issue, your comment, place, map link, time in IST, photo links when available, and “Kindly resolve at the earliest.” — it no longer adds an app branding line). You must tick “Yes, this portal/email is correct” (or use Yes, this is correct — continue) before anything happens. Continue then copies the text and opens the portal; Email department opens your mail app with that body, location and map link, and tries to attach the report photo(s) and a small video when the files are on the phone or can be downloaded — if the mail composer is unavailable (web / no mail app), it falls back to a mailto link with photo URLs in the body. If routing looks wrong, cancel and file on CPGRAMS (link in that screen). Share and Post on X skip the confirm step. The app is not affiliated with those portals.

Who can use Send / Share / Email / Post on X?

The report owner, and any active admin on any report.

Does Email department attach photos?

On Android/iOS, when a mail app is available, Email department opens a draft to the verified official address with the complaint body (including location and Google Maps link) and attaches local report photos. If a photo is only on Cloudinary, the app downloads it to a temporary cache first. A video is attached only when the file is reasonably small (about 8 MB or less). On web, or if no mail app is available, it falls back to mailto with photo links in the text instead of file attachments.

What is complaint status tracking?

On the report you can set Not sent, Sent (optional reference number and date), Resolved, No response or Rejected. Only the owner sets this for their report; an admin may set it too, and that change is written with an admin activity log entry. Statuses are entered by users and are not verified by the developer. After about 30 days with no reply while Sent / No response, the app offers an escalate link toward CPGRAMS / portal appeal options.

What is the department scorecard?

On the community page for a body or department (not the global view), the app shows counts of reports by complaint status and a simple responsiveness percentage based on those statuses.

Feed, flags & moderation

Who can see my posts?

Anyone using the app can see public ratings and reports with your username, comment, place, scores or category, and uploaded photos/videos. Your email, phone, legal name and birthday are never shown on the feed.

How does flagging work?

On someone else’s report you can Flag and pick a reason (once per report). Flagged items go to the admin queue. Reports with 3 or more flags are hidden from the community feed. Admins can clear flags, delete posts, ban or unban users, or delete a profile — every such action is recorded in an immutable admin activity log.

Is there a swear-word filter?

Yes. Before posting, comments are checked against a local list of common abusive words (English and common Hindi/Kannada transliterations). Strong criticism is fine; abuse is blocked with a polite message.

What if my account is banned?

A banned user cannot post new ratings or reports or flag content. Contact helpme.dev@outlook.com if you believe this was a mistake.

Permissions, offline & retention

Which permissions does the app ask for?

Location (to detect State and geotag evidence), Camera (photos/video), Microphone (sound on report videos, asked when you first record), and optional biometrics/Face ID for app unlock. Denying location blocks geotagged capture and State detection; denying camera blocks evidence; denying mic records video without sound.

Does the app work offline?

You need a network for sign-in, cloud posts and photo upload. If the profile cannot load (for example offline), the app shows an error with retry instead of sending you to create a new profile. Demo/mock builds without the cloud database keep data only on the device.

How long are posts kept?

Ratings and reports expire after about 2 years and are then removed. You can delete your own posts earlier. Cloudinary media may remain briefly after a report is deleted until removed manually.
